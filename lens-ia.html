Case study · Process intelligence

Lens information architecture

Lens turns recorded process history into a live process map. Everything a user does happens in one workspace. The map stays in the centre, and analysis opens around it in drawers and modals, so the user never loses sight of the process.

01 · Structure at a glance

One entry point, one workspace, four layers

Users come from the process list into a single Process Map workspace. Inside it, depth increases from the persistent frame to the map, then to drawers beside the map, and finally to modals that take over the screen.

04 · Principles

Why it is organised this way

The map is home

Every insight can point back to the map. Clicking a path, task or case highlights it in place instead of opening a new page.

Depth by surface

Hover shows a quick read, a drawer holds analysis, and a modal is only for focused tasks like replaying a session or importing data.

Summary before detail

Insights opens on a short summary. The Paths tab and the expanded dashboard are there for people who want to go further.

One filter state

Filters and the variants control shape the data everywhere at once: the map, the KPIs, Insights and Automation all read the same filtered cases.
