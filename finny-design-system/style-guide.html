Colour

A deep-forest primary ramp, a cool green-grey neutral ramp, and three semantic hues each paired with a tint. Components reference semantic tokens (--fy-text-heading), never raw primitives. Swatches marked added are not in the PDF and exist to give each status colour an AA-safe text variant.

Typography

One family, Poppins, in four weights. Sizes and weights match the PDF; line heights are proposed (1.25 headings, 1.4 subtitles, 1.5 body). Headings default to --fy-text-heading.

Space & shape

A 4px grid. Screens use a 16px side gutter (space-4) and 12px between stacked cards (space-3). Corners stay small: 6px on controls, 8px on cards.

Buttons

Full-width, 48px tall, 6px radius, label in Subtitle 1 (Semibold 16). Use one primary per screen, pinned to the bottom. Pair it with a secondary for a lesser action (“Edit All” over “Looks Good”).

Accessibility

Notes on the source PDF

Where the PDF was inconsistent, the tokens follow the rendered artwork rather than the labels.

Both “BG” swatches are labelled #02372F. The green one renders as #4C9B66 (same as success) and the light one as #F5F6F0. They are tokenised as bg-brand and bg-canvas.
“Subtitle 2” appears twice (Medium 16 and Medium 12). The 12px style is renamed subtitle-3. It is the field-label style.
Success tint is labelled #CCE6D4 but renders as #EEF7F1. The selected-chip fill also uses #EEF7F1, so both are kept: green-200 #CCE6D4 and green-50 #EEF7F1 (success-subtle).
Not specified in the PDF, so these are proposed: line heights, the spacing scale, radii, shadows, focus rings, gradient angles and component heights. Heights and radii were measured from the artwork at a 360px mobile frame (48px buttons, 40px inputs, 6px corners).
